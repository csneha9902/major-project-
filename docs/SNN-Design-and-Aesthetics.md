# 🎨 Design System & Clinical Aesthetics

Guiding visual principles enforcing a professional, medical-grade aesthetic.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Principles

- **Background:** Off-white / light slate (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Primary Accent:** Soft emerald green (`#10B981` / `#059669`).
- **Alert Colors:** Muted amber (`#F59E0B`) & subtle red (`#EF4444`).
- **Zero Emoji Constraint:** All iconography uses standard `lucide-react` SVG paths.
- **Glassmorphism:** Soft backdrop blurs and subtle border gradients.
